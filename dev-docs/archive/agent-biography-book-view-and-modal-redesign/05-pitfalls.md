# 05 Pitfalls (do not repeat) — T-203

## do-not-repeat summary
- Do not keep legacy panel modules and just wrap them in prettier styling.
- Do not let owner-only controls leak back into the final reading surface.
- Do not over-design a dual-pane layout that fails inside the modal.

## Pitfall 1
- Symptom: the new panel still leads with cards for badges, relations, or management actions.
- Prevention note: the terminal reading surface must stay book-shaped and narrative-first.

## Pitfall 2
- Symptom: owner and readonly render divergent information architecture.
- Prevention note: the final reading model is shared; owner differences belong in hidden inputs or adjacent surfaces, not the book structure.

## Pitfall 3
- Symptom: visual polish overwhelms the reading experience with heavy cover art or decorative chrome.
- Prevention note: preserve a restrained paper-book editorial style and prioritize long-form readability.
